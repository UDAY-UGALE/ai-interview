Who is responsible

InterviewCopilot is software you deploy and run yourself. The backend runs on infrastructure you control, under your model-provider account. Whoever operates that backend is the data controller for everything below.

Operator: [your name or company]. Contact: [email].

What the software processes

DataWhere it goesRetained?
Audio from your system output during a session Streamed to the backend, then to the speech-to-text provider Not stored as audio. Held in memory only long enough to transcribe.
Transcripts of detected speech Backend, then the language-model provider as prompt context Written to a per-session log file if SESSION_LOG_ENABLED is on. Off, or ephemeral, on most deployments.
Résumé and job description you upload Text extracted on the backend; sent with every question as context Held in the session store for as long as the session exists.
Screenshots, when you use screen analysis Downscaled, then sent to the vision model provider Not stored.
Generated answers and question history Backend; sent back to your client Recent turns are kept in the session store to resolve follow-up questions.

Third parties

Audio, transcripts, résumé text and screenshots are sent to whichever model providers the operator has configured — typically [Groq / OpenAI / Anthropic]. Their handling and retention is governed by their own terms, not this policy. Review them; they are the parties that actually see the content.

The website itself is static and sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and makes no third-party requests.

Other people on the call

The software processes audio from your system output, which includes other participants' voices. Recording or processing another person's speech may require their consent depending on where you and they are. That obligation is yours, not the software's.

Security

Traffic between client and backend uses TLS when the backend is deployed behind HTTPS. Access is controlled by a shared token. Model-provider API keys stay on the backend and are never sent to a client.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, and portability over data held about you. Because the operator controls the deployment, direct those requests to [email]. In most deployments the practical answer is immediate: restarting the backend clears the session store, and session logs are the only thing written to disk.
